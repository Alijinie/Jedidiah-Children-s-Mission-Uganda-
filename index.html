Core Initiatives

Children's Home

Providing a safe haven for 89 children, ensuring their physical, social, and mental needs are met with three meals a day.

Study Center

Operating nine classes from preschool to middle school, benefiting over 180 children who cannot afford school fees.

Vocational School

Combating poverty through practical courses in tailoring, hair dressing, and food preparation to build lifelong self-reliance.

The JCM Advantage

Making a Measurable Difference

Through dedicated care and community support, Jedidiah Children's Mission Uganda (JCM)[cite: 2] creates lasting transformation for vulnerable lives.

Global Network

Operating in Uganda since 2017 and recognized as an official Non-Profit Organization in the US since 2020.

Holistic Welfare

Providing 3 meals daily, medical attention, and safe shelter for orphans, street children, and HIV-positive youth.

Accessible Education

Operating a study center bridging the gap for over 180 local children whose families cannot afford standard school fees.

Faith-Based Guidance

Rooted deeply in Christian principles, offering daily fellowship, Bible study, and moral support to build resilient spirits.

Guided By Vision & Compassion

Meet the dedicated founders driving the mission of Jedidiah Children's Mission Uganda (JCM).

Pst. Pius Kabiswa Kiryowa

Volunteer Founder (Uganda)

"Having navigated a challenging background as an orphan myself, my deepest desire in founding JCM in 2017 was to create the safe home and supportive environment that every vulnerable child deserves. We believe in changing lives through practical love and consistent education."
~ Pst. Pius Kabiswa Kiryowa

Kelsey Lininger

NPO Co-Founder (USA)

"In 2020, we formally recognized JCM as a Non-Profit Organization in the United States to build a bridge of international support. By partnering with generous donors worldwide, we empower the children of Uganda to break the cycle of poverty and step confidently into their futures."
~ Kelsey Lininger
